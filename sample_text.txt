Sparklehoof was the tiniest baby unicorn in the whole Rainbow Nebula, with a silver horn that sneezed glitter whenever she laughed and a fluffy rainbow tail that always got tangled around her own little legs. One morning, while playing hide-and-seek with bouncing moon butterflies near her family's Star Meadow, she accidentally wandered into a shiny little space capsule that looked exactly like a giant cookie jar. Before she could hop back out, WHOOSH! The door zipped shut, the engines hiccupped, burped loudly, and blasted the capsule far across the galaxy. Uh-oh, squeaked Sparklehoof, her eyes growing as round as planets. Just then, a soft pink balloon-like robot gently floated out from a storage compartment. It looked like a squishy marshmallow with tiny flapping ears, glowing button eyes, and little noodle arms. Greetings! I am Bobo Bubble, Artificial General Intelligence Model Infinity, it announced proudly before immediately bouncing off the ceiling because it had forgotten gravity settings again. Oops! I do that every Tuesday... even when it isn't Tuesday! Sparklehoof giggled so hard that glitter shot from her nose. Bobo scanned the stars. Mission updated. Step one: find your parents. Step two: don't explode. Step three: remember where I left my charging cookie. The little capsule drifted through swirling galaxies until it floated beside a gigantic floating fruit market built on a spinning asteroid where pineapples sang opera and bananas wore sunglasses. There they met their first new friend, Wizzle Wobble, a tiny green jelly alien who constantly changed shape whenever he got excited. Sometimes he looked like a pancake, sometimes a balloon animal, and once he accidentally turned into a teapot during introductions. Wizzle had once dreamed of becoming the greatest Galactic Explorer, but every spaceship captain laughed because he couldn't stop wobbling during takeoff. Nobody hires a jelly who bounces into the snack cupboard, he sighed. Hearing Sparklehoof's story, Wizzle puffed himself into the shape of a superhero. Then I'll help! Every explorer needs one amazing adventure! The three friends asked passing ships for rides by holding colorful signs that read, Small Heroes Need Lift. We Pay in Jokes! Most ships zoomed away, but finally an enormous turtle-shaped cargo cruiser stopped. Paris is in France. It's the capital. The captain, Granny Glorp, was a purple turtle alien with giant glasses, six hats stacked on her head, and a habit of falling asleep while talking. She loved collecting stories more than treasure, so she happily gave them a ride across the Glitter Belt, where comets played tag with each other. Suddenly, dark shadows filled the stars. Huge black battleships appeared with glowing red eyes painted across their fronts. They belonged to Lord Grumblegore, the most powerful villain in the universe. Grumblegore was enormous, with ten twisting horns, twelve arms, and a booming voice that sounded like thunder mixed with someone stepping on squeaky toys. He wanted to capture every magical creature because he believed laughter made everyone too difficult to control. His grumpy army, called the Gloom Troopers, marched everywhere wearing helmets shaped like upside-down soup bowls. Hand over the unicorn! roared Grumblegore. Granny Glorp immediately pretended to be asleep.
